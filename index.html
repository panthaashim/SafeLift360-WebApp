Training Objectives & Sequence Briefing

  1. Step into the 360° Warehouse: Use mouse drag or touch drag on mobile/tablets to pan 360° horizontally and 180° vertically.
  2. Identify the 6 Real Loads: Locate components ranging from 6.0 kg to 30.0 kg (EV Battery on Euro Pallet, Transmission in Cradle, Brake Rotors Carton, etc.).
  3. Choose Kinetic Postures: Evaluate Stoop vs. Squat vs. Drag options to observe real-time L5/S1 spinal vertebral compression forces.
  4. Achieve Compliance: Maintain compression well below the NIOSH 3,400 N Action Limit to earn your verified training certificate.
